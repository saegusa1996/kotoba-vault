# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project.
Contributors should discuss ideas and code in good faith, criticize work rather
than people, respect privacy and copyright, and avoid discriminatory or
threatening behavior.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method listed on the
public repository. Maintainers will handle reports confidentially and as
promptly as possible.
